# Living Campus World Environment & Botanical Ecology Index

This directory contains the botanical surveys, ecological design documents, wind and atmospheric studies, and visual verification dossiers for the **Living Campus World Environment** of the University of Dhaka across 8 distinct campus zones (216 living assets) in *SparkVision*.

## Core Documents

* **[LIVING_CAMPUS_VISUAL_DOSSIER.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/LIVING_CAMPUS_VISUAL_DOSSIER.md)**: **Master Environmental Visual Dossier** documenting the 10 living environment benchmark viewpoints (ENV_01–ENV_10), validating multi-tier canopy layering, PBR water and reflection dynamics, native flora, and zero-gizmo render quality.
* **[Campus_Environment_Art_Validation_Report.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/Campus_Environment_Art_Validation_Report.md)**: Compliance audit evaluating density (*Where Winds Meet*) and atmosphere/wind (*Ghost of Tsushima*).
* **[DU_Flora_Fauna_Ecological_Survey.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/DU_Flora_Fauna_Ecological_Survey.md)**: Comprehensive botanical survey of Dhaka University's native tree species.
* **[Campus_Tree_Inventory_And_Placement_Matrix.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/Campus_Tree_Inventory_And_Placement_Matrix.md)**: Spatial placement matrix and zone coordinates for all 216 environmental assets.
* **[Campus_Atmosphere_Weather_Wind_Lighting_Plan.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/Campus_Atmosphere_Weather_Wind_Lighting_Plan.md)**: Lighting, sky atmosphere, and volumetric weather design.
* **[WhereWindsMeet_Environmental_Benchmark_Study.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/WhereWindsMeet_Environmental_Benchmark_Study.md)**: Technical art study on environmental layering and foliage density.
* **[GhostOfTsushima_Wind_Foliage_Atmosphere_Study.md](file:///Users/atiaoishi/Downloads/sparkvision/Documentation/WorldEnvironment/GhostOfTsushima_Wind_Foliage_Atmosphere_Study.md)**: Hierarchical vertex shader wind dynamics and atmospheric tuning.
