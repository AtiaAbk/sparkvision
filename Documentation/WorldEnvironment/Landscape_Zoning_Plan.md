# SPARKVISION — LANDSCAPE ZONING PLAN
## Functional Typologies, Architectural Framing & Microclimate Character

---

### Executive Overview

The landscape of the University of Dhaka is not a monoculture. It consists of **eight distinct functional landscape typologies**, ranging from high-prestige formal imperial plinths to shaded colonial tree tunnels and sacred memorial plazas. 

This document defines the spatial rules, character, and species composition for each zone, enforcing the **Open Campus & Natural Density Override**.

### 2. Detailed Typology Specifications

#### Zone 1: Monument & Heritage-Building Framing
* **Locations**: Curzon Hall, Central Shaheed Minar, Burdwan House (Bangla Academy).
* **Landscape Identity**: Restrained, symmetrical, reverent; designed to showcase monument architecture.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Architectural sightlines must remain **100% unobstructed** from primary approach axes.
  * Curzon Hall: Framed exclusively by two slender Royal Palms (`Roystonea regia`) flanking the monumental portal, and low boxwood hedges (`Duranta repens`) along the plinth base.
  * Shaheed Minar: Sacred Peepal (*Ficus religiosa*) and Banyan (*Ficus benghalensis*) placed strictly on the perimeter buffer, at least 18m back from the marble altar.
  * Zero trees clipping walls, windows, roofs, or entrance stairs.

#### Zone 2: Campus Avenues & Roadside Boulevards
* **Locations**: University Avenue, Fuller Road Boulevard, Nilkhet-Shahbagh Road.
* **Landscape Identity**: The iconic collegiate "Tree Tunnel" providing shade to commuting students.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Linear plantings of mature *Albizia saman* (Rain Tree) and *Swietenia mahagoni* (Mahogany).
  * Spaced $20.0 - 25.0\text{ m}$ apart along road verges.
  * Trunks placed at least $4.5\text{ m}$ back from road kerb line.
  * Under-canopy clearance kept above $4.5\text{ m}$ for vehicular and pedestrian passage.

#### Zone 3: Open Lawns & Central Campus Greens
* **Locations**: Curzon Front Lawn, The Mall, Jagannath Hall Ground.
* **Landscape Identity**: Wide, sunlit, manicured tropical turf for walking, sitting, and recreation.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * $85\% - 95\%$ of the designated zone area is open grass with zero tree trunks.
  * Maintained short lawn grass ($3 - 6\text{ cm}$ visual height).
  * Peripheral planting only, preserving broad open skies and long-distance horizon vistas.
  * The Mall: Preserves two massive, isolated, ancient specimen Banyans as natural architectural sculptures.

#### Zone 4: Shaded Campus Gathering Chattars
* **Locations**: Hakim Chattar (Library), TSC Mor, Madhur Canteen yard.
* **Landscape Identity**: Informal, lively social spaces beneath light, fragrant shade trees.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Medium-height, airy shade trees: Neem (*Azadirachta indica*), Debdaru (*Polyalthia longifolia*), and Kathgolap (*Plumeria*).
  * Paved brick benches (*Puka Chottor*) built around tree bases with root wells.
  * High pedestrian throughput with 4m clear corridors between trees.

#### Zone 5: Botanical Pocket Groves
* **Locations**: Botany Department rear annex, Curzon Northwest corner.
* **Landscape Identity**: Dense, multi-layered ecological pockets showcasing biodiversity.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Vertical stratification: Canopy (Banyan), Sub-Canopy (Jarul, Kadam), Shrub (Gardenia), Ground (Ferns, Kashful).
  * Strictly bounded: Kept within designated botanical garden fences; never allowed to spill across roads or into classical courtyards.

#### Zone 6: Ponds & Riparian Wetlands
* **Locations**: Curzon Botanical Pond, Shahidullah Hall Lake.
* **Landscape Identity**: Historic reflective water bodies with stepped brick ghats and native aquatic flora.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Open water surface: At least $70\%$ of the water surface is clear reflective water.
  * Aquatic flora: Clustered water lilies (*Nymphaea nouchali* - Shapla) and floating pads around eastern and northern margins.
  * Shoreline trees: Moisture-loving Jarul (*Lagerstroemia speciosa*) and Kadam (*Neolamarckia cadamba*) set back 3m from water edge.
  * Southern edge: Anchored by the historic stepped brick Pukur Ghat.

#### Zone 7: Residential Quad Courtyards
* **Locations**: Salimullah Muslim (SM) Hall inner quad, Shahidullah Hall central court.
* **Landscape Identity**: Intimate, private British-colonial quadrangles with perimeter walkways.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Central manicured square lawn with small ornamental fruit trees (Mango, Guava, Lemon) in corners.
  * Zero trees blocking interior veranda arcades or residential room windows.

#### Zone 8: Background Environmental Zones
* **Locations**: Distant campus perimeter (Old High Court wall, Suhrawardy boundary, Railway boundary).
* **Landscape Identity**: Soft, atmospheric green backdrop providing visual containment.
* **Framing Rules**:
  * Dense silhouette layering using varied tree heights ($14\text{ m} - 24\text{ m}$).
  * Blended with volumetric height fog to establish deep atmospheric perspective.
