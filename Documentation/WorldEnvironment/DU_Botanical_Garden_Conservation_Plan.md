# DU BOTANICAL GARDEN CONSERVATION & LANDSCAPE PLAN
**Project**: SparkVision — Level 1 University of Dhaka Digital Twin  
**Subject**: Curzon Hall Botanical Garden (Historic Botanical Research Sanctuary established 1908)  
**Location**: Grounds directly fronting and surrounding the Curzon Hall science complex.

---

## 1. Historical & Scientific Context
The Botanical Garden at Curzon Hall is one of the oldest living scientific plant repositories in Bangladesh. Developed concurrently with Curzon Hall in the early 20th century under the guidance of British botanists and later nurtured by the legendary Department of Botany, University of Dhaka, it houses rare medicinal, economic, and ornamental flora collected from the Sundarbans mangrove forest, the Chittagong Hill Tracts, and the Madhupur Sal tract.

### Zone A: Central Ceremonial Promenade & Fountain Plaza
* **Paving**: Red-clay brick pavers arranged in double herringbone pattern, bordered by white-painted concrete curbs.
* **Central Fountain**: Octagonal heritage red-brick water basin ($8.0\text{ m}$ diameter) with a central tiered stone fountain jet.
* **Floral Framing**: Concentric ornamental flowerbeds featuring Scarlet Sage (*Salvia splendens*), Marigolds (*Tagetes erecta*), and Zinnias.

### Zone B: Eastern & Western Parterres (Botanical Lawns)
* **Lawn Care**: Manicured Bermuda grass (*Cynodon dactylon*) maintained in deep emerald green.
* **Specimen Trees**:
  - Historic Ginkgo Biloba and Cycad living fossils.
  - Scarlet Krishnachura (*Delonix regia*) framing the south-east corner.
  - Scented Champa (*Magnolia champaca*) and Hasnahena (*Cestrum nocturnum*) perfuming the air during evening hours.

### Zone C: Southern Bamboo Sanctuary & Buffer Grove
* **Species**: Clumped *Bambusa vulgaris* and *Bambusa tulda*.
* **Buffer Function**: Shields the historical garden from High Court Road traffic noise, creating a tranquil acoustic sanctuary for gameplay exploration.
