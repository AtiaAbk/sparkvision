# UNIVERSITY OF DHAKA — CAMPUS TREE INVENTORY
## Empirical Census Distribution, Dominant Species & Structural Audit

### 1. Master Census Baseline Data

* **Census Agency**: DU Arboriculture Centre, DU Environment Society & Bangladesh Society for Ecological Research Initiative.
* **Census Fieldwork Period**: 2024–2025; Official Publication: July 2026.
* **Geographic Extent**: Main Campus (Curzon, Nilkhet, Fuller Road, Arts Faculty, Suhrawardy boundary), ~600 acres.
* **Total Enumerated Trees**: 17,161
* **Total Documented Species**: 277 species
* **Total Botanical Families**: 62 families
* **Origin Profile**: 58% Indigenous / Native; 42% Introduced / Exotic

### 4. Safety Audit & Architectural Clearance Enforcement

The official DU Census identified **2,213 trees under potential structural hazard** (canopies pressing against fragile heritage masonry, roots destabilizing colonial foundations, branches touching power lines).

In SparkVision, this empirical finding is strictly incorporated into our **Clearance Rules**:
1. **Curzon Hall Front Facade**: Zero wall-clipping trees. The 28 wall-penetrating meshes identified in earlier blockouts have been permanently removed.
2. **Clearance Envelope**:
   * Minimum **12.0 meters** clearance from historic building facades for large canopy trees (*Albizia saman*, *Ficus benghalensis*).
   * Minimum **6.0 meters** clearance for medium flowering trees (*Delonix regia*, *Peltophorum pterocarpum*).
   * Minimum **3.5 meters** clearance for vertical slender palms (*Roystonea regia*, *Areca catechu*).
3. **Road Traversal Buffer**: All trunks are placed at least **4.0 meters** beyond road kerbs, with lower branches pruned above **4.5 meters** (450 cm) to ensure unobstructed player and vehicular movement.
