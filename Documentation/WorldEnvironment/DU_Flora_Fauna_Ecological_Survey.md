# UNIVERSITY OF DHAKA FLORA & FAUNA ECOLOGICAL SURVEY
**Location**: University of Dhaka Campus, Ramna / Shahbagh, Dhaka, Bangladesh  
**Ecological Classification**: Tropical Moist Deciduous Gangetic Plain Delta Ecosystem  
**Baseline Data Source**: Department of Botany, University of Dhaka — *Comprehensive Campus Tree Census & Ecological Survey* (277 plant species, 17,161 inventoried trees across 275 acres).

## 2. Campus Avifauna & Ambient Wildlife
The lush greenery and aquatic bodies of Dhaka University support a vibrant resident and migratory wildlife population:

1. **Bhat Shalik / Common Myna (*Acridotheres tristis*)**: Ubiquitous ground foragers in lawns and plazas; bold, inquisitive hops; metallic yellow eye-patches.
2. **Kani Bok / Indian Pond Heron (*Ardeola grayii*)**: Patient solitary hunters standing motionless along the muddy banks of Curzon Botanical Pond and Shahidullah Pond.
3. **Pati Kak / House Crow (*Corvus splendens*)**: Vocal, opportunistic scavengers perching on colonial parapets and high tree branches.
4. **Tuntuni / Common Tailorbird (*Orthotomus sutorius*)**: Tiny, hyperactive warblers darting through bamboo thickets and bougainvillea hedges.
5. **Ghash Foring / Dragonflies & Damselflies (*Libellulidae*)**: Iridescent blue, red, and golden dragonflies hovering over pond lily pads in sunlight.
6. **Projapoti / Butterflies (Common Mormon, Lime Butterfly, Grass Yellow)**: Drifting lazily across floral borders, fallen Krishnachura petals, and zinnia flowerbeds.
