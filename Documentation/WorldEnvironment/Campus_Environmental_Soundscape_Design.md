# CAMPUS ENVIRONMENTAL SOUNDSCAPE DESIGN
**Project**: SparkVision — Level 1 University of Dhaka Digital Twin  
**Aesthetic Benchmark**: *Ghost of Tsushima* Organic Audio Layering & *Where Winds Meet* Natural Acoustic Ambience  
**Scope**: 3D Positional Audio, Biophony, Geophony, and Cultural Anthropophony.

---

## 1. Acoustic Layers & Sound Hierarchy

```
[Layer 1: Distant City Rumble (Subtle Low Pass)]
   |
[Layer 2: Local Wind & Canopy Rustle (Modulated by Tree Proximity)]
   |
[Layer 3: Campus Biophony (Bird Calls, Cicadas, Frogs, Water Lapping)]
   |
[Layer 4: Cultural Micro-Sounds (Rickshaw Bells, Distant Tea Stall Radio, Footsteps)]
```

### A. Geophony: Wind & Water
* **Canopy Wind Rustle**:
  - Filtered white noise mixed with recorded bamboo clatter and broadleaf rustle.
  - Dynamically volume-attenuated based on player distance to tree canopy clusters.
* **Curzon Pond Water Ambience**:
  - Gentle water lapping against the brick ghat steps.
  - Subtle ripple sounds when dragonflies skim the surface.
* **Rain on Chhajjas & Terracotta Roofs**:
  - Distinctive hollow drumming sound of heavy monsoon drops striking fired clay tiles.

### B. Biophony: Bengal Avifauna & Insects
* **Common Myna Call (*Shalik*)**: Bright, chattering whistles and sharp squawks echoing between colonial brick colonnades.
* **Asian Koel (*Kokil*)**: Melodic rising two-note call (*ku-OO, ku-OO*) ringing out from high mango tree branches during spring/summer.
* **Pond Heron (*Bok*)**: Deep, guttural croak when taking flight from the reed beds.
* **Evening Cicadas (*Jhijhi Poka*)**: High-frequency rhythmic pulsing in the dense bamboo thickets, intensifying as twilight falls.

### C. Anthropophony: University Campus Ambience
* **Rickshaw Bells (*Tring-Tring*)**: Occasional distant double-strike brass bell ringing from the perimeter roads (Shahbagh / Nilkhet).
* **Old Ceilings Fans (*Kosh-Kosh*)**: Rhythmic whirring of vintage 3-blade electric fans audible when walking past open ground-floor laboratory windows.
* **Footsteps on Varied Substrates**:
  - Crisp hollow clack on red-brick herringbone pavers.
  - Soft muffled crunch on grass lawns and fallen dry leaf carpets.
  - Wet splash on rain-soaked asphalt and puddle edges.
