### Master Species Profiles

#### 1. Rain Tree / Samanea saman (*Albizia saman*)
* **Bengali Name**: রেইনট্রি (Raintree) / শিরীষ (Shirish)
* **Family**: Fabaceae | **Origin**: Introduced (Naturalized Neotropical, British colonial introduction ~1850)
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $16.0 - 22.0\text{ m}$ ($1600 - 2200\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $20.0 - 28.0\text{ m}$ ($2000 - 2800\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk Diameter (DBH)**: $1.2 - 2.0\text{ m}$
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Massive umbrella-shaped (flabelliform) broad canopy.
  * Bipinnate dark green leaves with 2–6 pairs of pinnae; leaflets fold together under cloudy skies and at dusk (nyctinasty).
  * Rough, deeply fissured dark greyish-brown bark.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Rich lush foliage; occasional late pink/crimson puff-ball flowers; wet bark turns rich charcoal-brown.
* **Campus Placement**: Roadside avenue framing along University Avenue and Fuller Road. Minimum $14\text{ m}$ setback from historic buildings.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Raintree_Avenue` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Raintree_Leaf`

#### 2. Krishnachura / Royal Poinciana (*Delonix regia*)
* **Bengali Name**: কৃষ্ণচূড়া (Krishnachura)
* **Family**: Fabaceae | **Origin**: Introduced (Madagascar, naturalized in Bengal ~19th century)
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $9.0 - 13.5\text{ m}$ ($900 - 1350\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $11.0 - 16.0\text{ m}$ ($1100 - 1600\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk Diameter (DBH)**: $0.6 - 0.9\text{ m}$
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Broad, umbrella-like spreading branches with light, elegant, feathery bipinnate leaves.
  * Smooth to slightly flaky pale brown bark.
  * Fiery scarlet-red flowers with 5 petals (one standard petal variegated with white and yellow).
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Peak flowering tapers off in June; July displays deep emerald-green feathery leaf canopies with scattered late scarlet blossoms and developing elongated green pods.
* **Campus Placement**: Perimeter garden parterres, open lawn corners. Kept well clear of Curzon's central portal sightline.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Krishnachura_Bloom` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Krishnachura_Red`

#### 3. Radhachura / Yellow Flame (*Peltophorum pterocarpum*)
* **Bengali Name**: রাধাচূড়া (Radhachura)
* **Family**: Fabaceae | **Origin**: Native to Indo-Malaya and coastal Bengal
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $11.0 - 15.0\text{ m}$ ($1100 - 1500\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $10.0 - 14.0\text{ m}$ ($1000 - 1400\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk Diameter (DBH)**: $0.7 - 1.0\text{ m}$
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Dense rounded canopy, feathery bipinnate foliage resembling Krishnachura but slightly larger leaflets.
  * Fragrant bright yellow flowers with crinkled petals held in erect terminal panicles.
  * Copper-red winged seed pods.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Second flowering flush during monsoon; clusters of golden-yellow flowers contrasting against copper pods.
* **Campus Placement**: Roadside avenue transitions, TSC perimeter.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Radhachura_Yellow` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Radhachura_Yellow`

#### 4. Bengal Banyan (*Ficus benghalensis*)
* **Bengali Name**: বটগাছ (Bot Gachh)
* **Family**: Moraceae | **Origin**: Native (Indian Subcontinent & Bangladesh)
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $15.0 - 22.0\text{ m}$ ($1500 - 2200\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $25.0 - 40.0\text{ m}$ ($2500 - 4000\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk Structure**: Multiple column-like aerial prop roots descending from horizontal lateral limbs.
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Massive monumental silhouette with leathery, glossy oval leaves ($10 - 20\text{ cm}$).
  * Light grey, smooth to fluted bark; dense labyrinth of aerial roots.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Rapid aerial root growth in humid air; pairs of ripe crimson-red figs attracting frugivorous birds.
* **Campus Placement**: The Mall central focal point, Curzon Northwest botanical perimeter. Never placed adjacent to narrow roads.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Banyan_Mature` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Banyan_Leaf`

#### 5. Royal Palm (*Roystonea regia*)
* **Bengali Name**: রয়েল পাম (Royal Palm)
* **Family**: Arecaceae | **Origin**: Introduced (Colonial institutional landscape marker)
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $14.0 - 18.0\text{ m}$ ($1400 - 1800\text{ UU}$)
  * **Crown Spread**: $6.0 - 7.5\text{ m}$ ($600 - 750\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk Diameter**: $0.5 - 0.65\text{ m}$ with swollen middle section.
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Smooth, columnar, cement-grey ringed trunk; distinctive emerald-green crownshaft ($1.5\text{ m}$).
  * 15–18 large plumose pinnate fronds forming a stately spherical crown.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Vibrant glossy fronds; new spear emerging vertically from center.
* **Campus Placement**: Exactly two iconic palms symmetrically flanking Curzon Hall main portal (`X = -3350, Y = ±850`), exactly matching historical reference photos `REF-01` and `REF-03`.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Palm_Coconut` (scaled vertically to $1.6\times$ with straight trunk) | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Palm`, `MI_Foliage_PalmFrond`

#### 7. Jarul / Pride of India (*Lagerstroemia speciosa*)
* **Bengali Name**: জারুল (Jarul)
* **Family**: Lythraceae | **Origin**: Native wetlands and moist forests of Bangladesh
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $8.0 - 12.0\text{ m}$ ($800 - 1200\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $7.0 - 10.0\text{ m}$ ($700 - 1000\text{ UU}$)
  * **Trunk**: Fluted, mottled peeling bark.
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Profuse crinkled lilac-to-purple flowers in upright pyramidal panicles ($20 - 40\text{ cm}$).
  * Oblong dark green leaves turning reddish before falling.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Spectacular monsoon flowering; petals falling onto water surfaces and moist soil.
* **Campus Placement**: Curzon Botanical Pond bank, Shahidullah Lake margin.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Jarul_Purple` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Jarul_Lilac`

#### 8. Neem (*Azadirachta indica*)
* **Bengali Name**: নিম (Neem)
* **Family**: Meliaceae | **Origin**: Native to Bangladesh and Indo-Burma
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $7.5 - 11.0\text{ m}$ ($750 - 1100\text{ UU}$)
  * **Canopy Spread**: $6.0 - 9.0\text{ m}$ ($600 - 900\text{ UU}$)
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Light, delicate, airy canopy with serrated falcate pinnate leaves.
  * Rough reddish-brown to dark grey fissured bark.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Dense aromatic green canopy; small olive-like green fruits ripening to yellow.
* **Campus Placement**: Hakim Chattar, Library breezeways, pedestrian rest nodes.
* **Unreal Asset**: `SM_Tree_Neem_Delicate` | **Materials**: `MI_Bark_Tree`, `MI_Foliage_Neem_Green`

#### 9. Bengal Clumping Bamboo (*Bambusa tulda*)
* **Bengali Name**: তুলদা বাঁশ (Tulda Baash)
* **Family**: Poaceae | **Origin**: Native to Bangladesh
* **Mature Dimensions**:
  * **Height**: $6.0 - 9.5\text{ m}$ ($600 - 950\text{ UU}$)
  * **Cluster Footprint**: $3.0 - 4.5\text{ m}$ diameter
  * **Culm Diameter**: $5.0 - 8.0\text{ cm}$
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Dense upright clumping habit; smooth green-to-golden culms with white nodal powder.
  * Delicate linear-lanceolate leaves swaying in wind corridors.
* **July Monsoon Phenology**: Rapid shoot emergence from underground rhizomes.
* **Campus Placement**: Designated botanical garden pockets and science department rear buffers. Never placed in front of historical facades or roads.
