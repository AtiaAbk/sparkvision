# DU POND & WATER-EDGE ECOLOGY DESIGN
**Project**: SparkVision — Level 1 University of Dhaka Digital Twin  
**Key Water Bodies**: 
1. **Curzon Hall Botanical Pond**: Historic rectangular stepped masonry tank ($85\text{ m} \times 45\text{ m}$) located southwest of Curzon Hall main building.
2. **Shahidullah Hall Lake / Dighi**: Historic natural freshwater tank ($110\text{ m} \times 60\text{ m}$) located south of Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah Hall.

---

## 1. Ecological & Visual Stratification of the Ponds

```
[Dry Lawn] -> [Terracotta Brick Paved Path] -> [Stepped Ghat / Muddy Bank] -> [Emergent Reeds] -> [Floating Lily Pads] -> [Open Water]
```

### A. Aquatic Surface Layer (Water Shader & Flora)
* **Water Material Configuration (`M_Water_CurzonPond`)**:
  - Translucent PBR water shader with depth-based color absorption (shallow emerald green `#1A3B2B` fading to deep murky slate `#0D1E18` at $2.5\text{ m}$ depth).
  - Subtle planar normal map wave ripples driven by the global wind vector.
  - Specular highlights capturing reflections of Curzon Hall's red brick towers and the sky.
* **Floating Macrophytes**:
  - **Shapla / White Water Lily (*Nymphaea nouchali*)**: Floating flat circular notched pads ($30\text{ cm} - 50\text{ cm}$ diameter) scattered in organic clusters along the perimeter, with upright white and pale violet blossoms.
  - **Water Hyacinth (*Eichhornia crassipes*)**: Bulbous floating rosettes with pale lavender-blue flower spikes clustering along sheltered corners.

### B. Emergent & Marginal Littoral Zone (Shoreline)
* **Vegetation Types**:
  - **Bengal Reeds (*Phragmites karka*) & Kashful (*Saccharum spontaneum*)**: Tall feathery plumes ($1.8\text{ m} - 2.5\text{ m}$) growing in dense linear stands along unpaved pond margins.
  - **Wild Arum / Kochu (*Colocasia esculenta*)**: Broad heart-shaped elephant-ear leaves ($60\text{ cm} - 90\text{ cm}$) sprouting from wet mud banks.
  - **Sedge Grass (*Cyperus rotundus*)**: Clumped dark green grass tufts stabilizing the muddy water transition.

### C. Riparian Tree Canopy (Overhanging Pond Banks)
* **Weeping Willow / Kadam / Jarul**:
  - *Lagerstroemia speciosa* (Jarul) extending flowering purple branches over the water.
  - *Neolamarckia cadamba* (Kadam) dropping fragrant golden globes that float on the water surface.
  - Leaning *Cocos nucifera* (Coconut palms) arcing outward over the water, casting dramatic diagonal reflections.

## 2. Historic Stepped Brick Ghats (*Pukur Ghat*)
Both Curzon Pond and Shahidullah Pond feature traditional brick *ghats* (masonry staircases descending into the water):
* **Stepped Descent**: 6 tiers of weathered red brick steps ($20\text{ cm}$ rise, $45\text{ cm}$ tread) descending below the waterline.
* **Weathering & Submerged Decals**:
  - Steps below water are coated in dark green slippery algae and moss.
  - Surface steps exhibit weathered lime mortar joints with small maidenhair ferns (*Adiantum*) sprouting between cracks.
* **Atmospheric Purpose**: Historic gathering and resting spot for students, poets, and faculty.
