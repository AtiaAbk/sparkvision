# UNIVERSITY OF DHAKA MICROCLIMATE & SEASONAL ECOLOGY REPORT
**Location**: Dhaka Metropolitan Area (Latitude $23.73^\circ\text{ N}$, Longitude $90.40^\circ\text{ E}$)  
**Climatic Zone**: Humid Subtropical Monsoon Climate (Köppen *Cwa* / *Aw*)  
**Environmental Significance**: Dhaka University acts as a massive "Urban Green Island", lowering ambient temperatures by $2.5^\circ\text{C} - 3.8^\circ\text{C}$ relative to the surrounding dense concrete metropolis of Dhaka.

## 2. Seasonal Dynamic Cycle (Bengal Six Seasons / *Shoro Ritu*)
For SparkVision dynamic environment lighting, atmosphere, and particle systems:

1. **Grishma (Summer — April to May)**:
   - Blazing sunlight, temperature $32^\circ\text{C} - 38^\circ\text{C}$.
   - Krishnachura and Radhachura trees explode in vibrant scarlet and golden-yellow blooms across campus.
   - Sudden pre-monsoon *Kalbaishakhi* (Nor'wester) storms with dramatic dark anvil clouds, howling winds, and flying leaf whirlwinds.
2. **Barsha (Monsoon — June to August)**:
   - Daily torrential rains, lush saturated green vegetation, temperature $28^\circ\text{C} - 32^\circ\text{C}$.
   - Kadam flowers bloom into fragrant orange spheres; ponds brim with water; moss and ferns cover every brick plinth.
   - Atmosphere characterized by heavy volumetric mist, wet asphalt specular reflections, and dripping canopy audio.
3. **Sharat (Early Autumn — September to October)**:
   - Clear azure skies with fluffy white cumulus clouds; gentle breezes; temperature $26^\circ\text{C} - 30^\circ\text{C}$.
   - White feathery *Kashful* (*Saccharum spontaneum*) reeds sway along pond edges and open fields.
4. **Hemanta (Late Autumn — November)**:
   - Mild golden afternoon sun; cool evenings; falling dry leaves forming crisp rustling ground carpets.
5. **Sheet (Winter — December to January)**:
   - Cool, dry, temperature $12^\circ\text{C} - 22^\circ\text{C}$.
   - Morning dense white fog rolling across Curzon lawns and Curzon pond until mid-morning sun burns it away.
6. **Basanta (Spring — February to March)**:
   - Gentle warm breezes; fresh bright-green leaf shoots emerge; mango trees covered in aromatic blossoms (*Mukul*).
