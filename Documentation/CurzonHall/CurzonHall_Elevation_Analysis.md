# CURZON HALL ELEVATION ANALYSIS & FACADE COMPOSITION
**Project**: SparkVision — Level 1 University of Dhaka Digital Twin  
**Subject**: South, North, East, and West Facade Elevations  
**Key Feature**: Rhythmic Colonnades, Multi-Tier Arches, Horizontal String Courses & Overhanging Chhajjas.

---

## 1. South Elevation (Principal Public Facade)
The South Elevation is the primary visual face of Curzon Hall and the monumental centerpiece of Level 1.

### Tripartite Horizontal Division:
1. **The Base / Plinth ($0.0\text{ m} - 0.8\text{ m}$)**:
   - Built on a continuous dressed red-brick plinth with a bevelled white sandstone torus moulding.
   - Five broad stone steps lead up to the central entrance portal.
2. **The Ground Storey ($0.8\text{ m} - 7.2\text{ m}$)**:
   - Pierced by 24 horseshoe arch openings (12 west, 12 east) organized in pairs within 12 modular bays.
   - Massive masonry piers ($85\text{ cm}$ thick) impart an impression of immense structural stability and gravitas.
   - Lower *chhajja* (eave) projects $1.2\text{ m}$ outward at $+7.2\text{ m}$, casting a sharp shadow line separating the floors.
3. **The Upper Storey ($7.2\text{ m} - 14.2\text{ m}$)**:
   - Pierced by 24 multi-cusped scalloped arch openings (7 lobes each), creating a light, airy, lace-like contrast against the heavy base.
   - Paired octagonal colonnettes with carved lotus capitals support each cusped arch springing point.
   - Pierced white jali balustrades line the floor level ($+7.2\text{ m}$ to $+8.2\text{ m}$).
   - Upper *chhajja* projects $1.2\text{ m}$ outward at $+13.0\text{ m}$.
   - Parapet wall ($+13.0\text{ m}$ to $+14.2\text{ m}$) crowned with stepped pointed merlons.

## 2. Central Entrance Portal Elevation
* **Width**: $18.0\text{ m}$, projecting $4.8\text{ m}$ south from the wing facade.
* **Monumental Grand Iwan Arch**:
  - Vaulted arch opening spanning from ground level up to $+13.5\text{ m}$.
  - Framed by a rectangular *pishtaq* frame with raised white plaster borders and calligraphic-style ornamental friezes.
  - Arch profile: 9-lobed cusped arch with recessed concentric moldings.
* **Second Tier Pediment ($+14.2\text{ m} - +19.8\text{ m}$)**:
  - Triple arched gallery window with timber louvers.
  - White sandstone clock dial medallion set in a carved red terracotta frame.
  - Stepped parapet coping terminating in twin octagonal minarets ($+21.2\text{ m}$).
* **Rooftop Twin Chhatris ($+19.8\text{ m} - +23.4\text{ m}$)**:
  - Positioned symmetrically on the left and right flanks of the portal roof terrace.
  - 8-column open-air pavilions with bulbous fluted domes and brass kalasa finials.

---

## 3. Corner Octagonal Tower Elevations (East & West)
* **Footprint**: Octagonal drum, $7.2\text{ m}$ across opposing flats.
* **Height**: $25.2\text{ m}$ from ground level to finial apex (the tallest architectural features on campus).
* **Storey Articulation**:
  - *Tier 1 (Ground to $+7.2\text{ m}$)*: Blind octagonal masonry base with narrow arrow-slit lancet windows.
  - *Tier 2 ($+7.2\text{ m}$ to $+14.2\text{ m}$)*: Arched louvered windows on alternating facets framed with white plaster trim.
  - *Tier 3 ($+14.2\text{ m}$ to $+19.5\text{ m}$)*: Corbelled observation balcony projecting $0.6\text{ m}$ with pierced geometric jali balustrades.
  - *Tier 4 ($+19.5\text{ m}$ to $+25.2\text{ m}$)*: Open-air Rajput chhatri kiosk with 8 octagonal pillars, multi-bracketed chhajja eave, hemispherical cupola dome, and brass kalasa finial.

---

## 4. North Elevation (Courtyard Elevation)
* Mirroring the South Elevation in rhythm and structural bays, but enclosing the inner courtyard.
* Features twin rear stairwell towers ($18.5\text{ m}$ height) projecting into the courtyard to provide vertical egress.
* Covered walkways provide sheltered access to the Chemistry lecture theatres and Physics laboratories.
