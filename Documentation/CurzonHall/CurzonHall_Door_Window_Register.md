## 2. Joinery Details & Environmental Weathering
* **Louvered Shutters (*Jhilmil*)**:
  - Angled teak slats tilted at $45^\circ$ downward to deflect direct sun glare and rain while admitting natural coastal breezes.
  - Weathered dark walnut stain (`#2C1F17`) with subtle edge wear and moisture desaturation near the sill.
* **Fanlights (*Transom Windows*)**:
  - Radial sunburst pattern iron and wood glazing bars.
  - Aged antique glass panes with subtle green tint and micro-roughness variations ($0.15$ PBR Roughness).
* **Perforated *Jali* Screens**:
  - Authentic Mughal geometric interlace patterns.
  - Provides realistic dynamic shadow casting when illuminated by the dynamic Unreal Engine directional sunlight and Lumen GI.
