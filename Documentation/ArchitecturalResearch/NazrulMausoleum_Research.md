## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Adjacent to the University of Dhaka Central Mosque, situated along Kazi Nazrul Islam Avenue between Doyel Chattar and Shahbagh.
* **Functional Role**: Final resting place of Bangladesh's National Poet, **Kazi Nazrul Islam** (the "Rebel Poet" / *Bidrohi Kobi*), fulfilling his famous song lyric: *"Bury me beside a mosque, so I can hear the Azan..."*

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Open-Air Memorial Cenotaph with Traditional Islamic Jali Screens**.
* **Significance**: Following his death on August 29, 1976, the poet was buried with full state honors directly beside the DU Central Mosque, creating one of the most spiritually revered historical landmarks on campus.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Dignified, open-sky low-rise memorial enclosed by elegant perforated screens, maintaining clear visual connection to the adjacent white dome of the Central Mosque.
