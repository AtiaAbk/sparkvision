# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: RAJU MEMORIAL SCULPTURE

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: At the center of the TSC Mor roundabout, intersecting University Avenue, Fuller Road, and Mall Road.
* **Functional Role**: Primary symbol of democratic student solidarity, freedom of speech, and anti-terrorism resistance in Bangladesh.

## 2. Geographic Coordinates
* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.731700° N, 90.392900° E`
* **Metric CRS (UTM Zone 46N / EPSG:32646)**: Easting = `234,209.12 m`, Northing = `2,626,946.10 m`
* **Unreal Engine Location**: `(X = 41500, Y = -147500, Z = 20)`

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Pedestal Base**: Stepped circular/hexagonal concrete plinth, diameter $\approx 10.0\text{ m}$, height $\approx 1.8\text{ m} - 2.0\text{ m}$.
* **Sculptural Figures**: Eight life-sized human figures standing approximately $2.1\text{ m} - 2.4\text{ m}$ tall above the plinth. Total monument height $\approx 4.2\text{ m} - 4.5\text{ m}$.
* **Span of Joined Arms**: The linked-arm chain of 8 figures creates a semicircular forward arc spanning $\approx 7.5\text{ m}$.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Figurative Expressionist Public Monument**.
* **Artists**: Designed and sculpted by **Shyamal Chowdhury** and **Gopal Paul**.
* **Inauguration**: September 17, 1997.
* **Historical Dedication**: Built in honor of **Moin Hossain Raju**, a leader of the Bangladesh Students' Union who was martyred on March 13, 1992, while leading a peaceful anti-terrorism demonstration against violent armed student factions.
* **Real-Life Models**: All eight figures were sculpted from real student leaders and friends of Raju (including his brother Muneem Hossain Rana).

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Dynamic, forward-leaning human wall with tightly linked arms, conveying unshakable solidarity, collective courage, and moral defiance.

## 6. Facade & Radial Arrangement
* **Orientation**: Positioned in an open arc facing east towards the incoming roadway from Shaheed Minar and Curzon Hall, greeting approaching students.

## 7. Roofline / Crown
* **Crown**: The heads of the eight figures are held high at slightly varied heights ($2.1\text{ m} - 2.4\text{ m}$), creating an organic, non-uniform human silhouette against the sky.

## 8. Plinth & Base Design
* **Base Construction**: Stepped reinforced concrete terraces allowing citizens and students to sit, place flowers, or gather during protest rallies.

## 9. Ornamentation & Surface Sculpting
* **Artistic Treatment**: Expressive chiseling, textured drapery on casual student attire (shirts, trousers, kurtas), and intense facial expressions of resolute determination.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Sculpture Material**: Textured cast bronze / patinated copper finish over reinforced cement core ($0.45$ Roughness, $0.65$ Metallic, `#3D483A` with green verdigris accents).
* **Base Material**: Smooth fair-faced white/grey polished terrazzo concrete.

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Roadway Island**: Circular paved traffic island with curb stones and safety stanchions.
* **Context**: Flanked by mature trees surrounding TSC and the bustling road junction.
