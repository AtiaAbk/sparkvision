# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: DU CENTRAL LIBRARY

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: North of Mall Road and east of Arts Faculty building, central university campus.
* **Functional Role**: Primary academic research library and archival repository of the University of Dhaka.

## 2. Geographic Coordinates
* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.732500° N, 90.393600° E`
* **Metric CRS (UTM Zone 46N / EPSG:32646)**: Easting = `234,279.80 m`, Northing = `2,627,034.40 m`
* **Unreal Engine Location**: `(X = 48000, Y = -140000, Z = 20)`

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Footprint**: Large rectilinear 3-storey reading and stack block spanning $\approx 75.0\text{ m} \times 40.0\text{ m}$.
* **Height**: Multi-storey structure, total building height $\approx 14.5\text{ m} - 16.0\text{ m}$.
* **Brise-Soleil Projections**: Concrete sun-breaker louvers projecting $0.8\text{ m} - 1.2\text{ m}$ outward from the glass curtain wall.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Pioneering Bengali Modernism / Situated Tropical Modernism**.
* **Architect**: Designed by **Muzharul Islam** (1953–1954), celebrated as the father of modern architecture in Bangladesh and student of Paul Rudolph and Louis Kahn.
* **Design Philosophy**: "Building in the Park" (*bagan-bari* concept). Rejection of colonial mimicry and orientalist revivalism; pure geometric expression adapted to monsoon rains, humidity, and tropical glare.
* **Adjacent Cultural Epicenter**: Fronted by **Hakim Chattar**, the famous intellectual tea and debate circle of Dhaka University students and writers.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Clean, floating horizontal cubist mass elevated on exposed concrete pilotis, punctuated by rhythmic vertical and horizontal louvers that create dramatic shadow patterns.

## 6. Facade Arrangement
* **Modularity**: Repetitive structural bay rhythm ($6.0\text{ m}$ module spacing).
* **Deep Louvers**: Interlocking horizontal and vertical concrete fins creating a protective thermal envelope around the glazed reading rooms.

## 7. Roofline
* **Roof**: Deep cantilevered flat concrete slab with recessed penthouse mechanics and perimeter drainage gutters.

## 8. Windows & Entrance
* **Sun Protection**: Floor-to-ceiling recessed glass behind structural concrete fins, preventing blinding tropical light while ensuring continuous natural daylight.
* **Entrance Foyer**: Shaded double-height open entrance plaza underneath the pilotis.

## 9. Structural Columns & Pilotis
* **Structure**: Exposed reinforced concrete frame (*ossature indépendante*).
* **Pilotis**: Cylindrical and chamfered rectangular pillars supporting the upper reading halls, allowing campus pathways to flow beneath the building.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Fair-Faced Concrete**: Smooth architectural concrete with faint timber shuttering grain ($0.75$ Roughness, `#9E9891`).
* **Terracotta Brick Accents**: Selected side facade panels finished in unplastered local red brick ($0.88$ Roughness).
* **Glass**: Low-reflectance solar-control tinted glazing.

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Ecological Integration**: Built deliberately around existing century-old Banyan and Rain Trees, preserving the lush botanical canopy.
* **Hakim Chattar**: Shaded brick-paved courtyard with low concrete benches and traditional campus tea stalls.
