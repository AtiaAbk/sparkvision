# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: CURZON HALL

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Southern sector of Dhaka University campus, Shahbagh / Ramna, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Situated adjacent to High Court premises and north of Dhaka Medical College.
* **Functional Role**: Epicenter of Faculty of Science and Faculty of Biological Sciences, University of Dhaka.

## 3. Building Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Total Length (East-West)**: Approximately $140.0\text{ m} - 150.0\text{ m}$ including east and west pavilion projections.
* **Central Entrance Portico**: Width $\approx 16.0\text{ m} - 17.0\text{ m}$, Depth $\approx 4.5\text{ m} - 5.0\text{ m}$, Height to parapet $\approx 19.5\text{ m} - 20.0\text{ m}$.
* **Veranda Colonnade Bays**: Modular bay width $\approx 8.0\text{ m} - 8.2\text{ m}$, Bay depth $\approx 2.0\text{ m} - 2.5\text{ m}$, Height per storey $\approx 7.0\text{ m}$. Total 2-storey height $\approx 14.2\text{ m}$.
* **Corner Towers**: Width/Diameter $\approx 7.2\text{ m}$, Tower masonry height $\approx 16.6\text{ m}$, Chhatri kiosk height $\approx 8.6\text{ m}$, Total tower height $\approx 25.2\text{ m}$.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: Textbook masterpiece of **Indo-Saracenic Revival architecture**, fusing European Renaissance spatial planning and Gothic structural lines with traditional Mughal and Sultanate motifs.
* **Historical Origins**: Foundation stone laid on February 19, 1904, by Lord Curzon (Viceroy of India). Financed by the Nawabs of Bhawal. Intended as a town hall / college library; served as Eastern Bengal & Assam capital hall (1905–1911); incorporated into Dhaka University on July 1, 1921.
* **Historical Significance**: Historic birthplace of Bengali intellectual and political resistance (anti-Urdu declaration protests in 1948; Satyendra Nath Bose's research on Quantum Statistics).

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Symmetrical grand horizontal massing anchored by a prominent two-storey projecting central portal, flanked by deep double-tiered verandas and bookended by tall octagonal corner towers topped with hemispherical domed chhatris.
* **Dome Ground Truth**: **Definitively flat roofline with stepped merlons and kiosk domes**. No oversized central onion dome exists on Curzon Hall.

## 6. Facade Arrangement
* **Symmetry**: Strong bilateral symmetry along the north-south axis.
* **Bays**: Central 16m portal flanked by 6 modular veranda bays to the west and 6 modular veranda bays to the east.
* **Horizontal Articulation**: Continuous white sandstone string courses and projecting chhajja eaves separate the ground floor and second floor.

## 7. Roofline
* **Flat Terrace Roof**: Flat accessible roof terrace surrounded by continuous stepped parapet merlons (crenellations).
* **Roof Pavilions (Chhatris)**: 8-pillared open-air domed kiosks placed symmetrically on corner towers and over the central portico parapet.

## 8. Windows & Entrance Design
* **Central Portal**: High monumental arch opening spanning two stories, featuring a multi-foil cusped arch with molded white sandstone arch rings.
* **Ground Floor Fenestration**: Rusticated segmental arches with deep timber louvered casement windows.
* **Upper Floor Fenestration**: Pointed multi-foil arches opening into shaded continuous verandas.

## 9. Columns, Arches, Verandas & Ornamentation
* **Columns**: Paired slender octagonal columns with carved lotus-flower capitals on upper verandas.
* **Arches**: Sturdy rusticated brick piers on ground floor; delicate cusped 7-lobed and 9-lobed arches on second floor.
* **Balustrades**: Geometric terracotta and white-painted perforated jali screens lining upper balconies.
* **Brackets**: Carved stone and brick cantilever brackets supporting projecting chhajja drip-eaves.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Red Brick Masonry**: Custom locally fired terracotta bricks with weathered lime mortar joints ($0.85$ PBR Roughness, `#84291F`).
* **White Sandstone Trim**: Smooth limestone / plaster dressing on arch moldings, capitals, and cornices ($0.70$ Roughness, `#E5DEC9`).
* **Woodwork**: Dark weathered teak wood on louvered shutters and interior doors.
* **Ironwork**: Black cast-iron railings and heritage lantern fixtures ($0.85$ Metallic).

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Front (South)**: Expansive herringbone red-brick pedestrian promenade flanked by manicured botanical lawns and floral borders.
* **Botanical Pond**: Historic rectangular pond with water lilies and stone borders situated southwest of the main promenade.
* **Flora**: Giant Rain Trees (*Samanea saman*), Banyan, and Neem trees framing the eastern and western compound flanks.
