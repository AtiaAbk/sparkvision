# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: CENTRAL SHAHEED MINAR

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Southeastern sector of University of Dhaka campus, near Dhaka Medical College and Jagannath Hall.
* **Functional Role**: National monument commemorating the Language Movement martyrs of February 21, 1952; the spiritual symbol of the Bengali language and sovereignty.

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Height of Central Mother Pillar**: Exactly **14.0 meters (46.0 feet)** tall.
* **Forward Slant of Mother Pillar**: Inclined forward at an angle of approximately $15^\circ - 18^\circ$.
* **Crimson Sun Disc**: Vertical circular disc, diameter $\approx 5.0\text{ m} - 5.5\text{ m}$, mounted directly behind the central pillar.
* **Flanking Child Pillars**: 4 pillars arranged symmetrically in two pairs:
  - Inner pair: Height $\approx 10.0\text{ m}$.
  - Outer pair: Height $\approx 7.5\text{ m} - 8.0\text{ m}$.
* **Raised Marble Dais (Podium)**: Stepped terrace measuring approximately $32.0\text{ m} \times 22.0\text{ m}$, elevated $1.2\text{ m}$ above the paved plaza.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Monumental Modernist Sculpture / Symbolic Memorial Architecture**.
* **Conceptual Designers & Sculptors**: Conceived and designed by pioneering artist **Hamidur Rahman** and sculptor **Novera Ahmed** in 1957.
* **Symbolism**: The central leaning pillar represents the mourning yet steadfast **Mother**, while the four flanking pillars represent her **Sacrificed Sons** who laid down their lives for their mother tongue. The brilliant crimson sun disc represents the dawn of linguistic and national awakening bathed in martyr blood.
* **Historical Chronology**: Temporary student monument erected Feb 23, 1952, demolished by police; permanent structure built 1957–1963; dynamited and leveled by the Pakistan Army during *Operation Searchlight* on March 25, 1971; rebuilt and expanded to present dimensions in 1983.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: One of the most famous silhouettes in South Asia: a towering central angled pylon framed by step-down flanking columns and a vivid blood-red circular backdrop.

## 6. Symmetrical Arrangement
* **Bilateral Symmetry**: Strict, solemn axial symmetry centered on the north-south axis.
* **Radial Curve**: The 5 pillars are arranged along a gentle concave arc opening outward toward the public mourning square.

## 7. Vertical Rhythm & Pillar Tops
* **Pillar Terminations**: The tops of the pillars are horizontally cut with clean rectangular profiles. The central pillar's forward incline gives it a protective, embracing gesture.

## 8. Altar Platform & Processional Steps
* **Podium**: White polished marble dais accessed by a wide ceremonial staircase of 7 broad low risers.
* **Base Floor**: Adorned with black marble boundary trim.

## 9. Ornamentation & Poetic Railings
* **Wrought-Iron Rails**: Flanking walkways feature low black wrought-iron railings cast with immortal lines of Bengali poetry honoring the martyrs (by poets Rabindranath Tagore, Kazi Nazrul Islam, and Abdul Gaffar Choudhury).
* **Mural Basement**: Beneath the dais lies the historical basement gallery intended for Language Movement murals.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Pillars & Podium**: Clad in stark, pristine white polished marble tiles ($0.35$ Roughness, `#F2EFE9`).
* **Crimson Sun Disc**: Matte crimson-red stucco finish ($0.80$ Roughness, `#9E1B1B`).
* **Granite Trim**: Polished black granite borders ($0.25$ Roughness, `#151515`).

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Memorial Plaza**: Large open red-brick and concrete tiled plaza designed for thousands of barefoot pilgrims during *Ekushey February* (*Prabhat Pheri*).
* **Perimeter Trees**: Tall Krishnachura and Banyan trees providing dignified shade and scarlet flower blossoms around the plaza borders.
