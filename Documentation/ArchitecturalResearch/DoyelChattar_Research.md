# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: DOYEL CHATTAR

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Prominent traffic roundabout situated at the northern exit of Curzon Hall, intersecting University Avenue, Kazi Nazrul Islam Avenue, and High Court road.
* **Functional Role**: Iconic transitional gateway connecting Old Dhaka to the main University of Dhaka academic enclave.

## 2. Geographic Coordinates
* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.727999° N, 90.400318° E`
* **Metric CRS (UTM Zone 46N / EPSG:32646)**: Easting = `234,967.35 m`, Northing = `2,626,530.86 m`
* **Unreal Engine Location**: `(X = 7507, Y = -16487, Z = 20)` (~412m North-West of Curzon Hall)

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Outer Roundabout Diameter**: Approximately $28.0\text{ m} - 30.0\text{ m}$ including roadway lanes.
* **Fountain Basin Diameter**: Octagonal water basin spanning $16.0\text{ m} - 18.0\text{ m}$ across flats.
* **Monument Base Height**: Raised concrete basin curb $\approx 0.6\text{ m}$ above road level.
* **Central Sculptural Monument**: Height $\approx 7.0\text{ m} - 8.5\text{ m}$ from water level to highest bird tail.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Public Civic Water Monument / Sculptural Fountain**.
* **Artist / Sculptor**: Designed and executed by master sculptor **Azizul Jalil Pasha**.
* **Theme**: Celebrates the national bird of Bangladesh, the **Oriental Magpie-Robin (*Doyel*)**, symbolizing natural freedom, musicality, and Bengali cultural identity.
* **Urban Character**: Traditional artisan clay pottery, bamboo crafts, and folk souvenir stalls line the pedestrian footpaths directly east of the roundabout.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Symmetrical octagonal base tapering upward into a dynamic pair of intertwined stylized birds with elevated plumage and raised tails, creating a sharp, recognizable vertical beacon against the sky.

## 6. Facade & Radial Symmetry
* **Geometry**: Octagonal geometry ($8\times 45^\circ$ segments) with perimeter water spillways and submerged fountain jet rings.
* **Dual Figures**: Two Doyel birds perching back-to-back, facing opposite cardinal directions to ensure visual interest from all 4 incoming avenues.

## 7. Roofline / Crest
* **Crest**: The pointed beaks and fanned tailfeathers of the two birds form the highest focal point at $+8.5\text{ m}$.

## 8. Basin & Fountain Mechanics
* **Basin**: Tiered concentric concrete octagons lined with turquoise ceramic tiles.
* **Water Jets**: Central vertical geyser nozzle surrounded by an outer ring of inward-angled bubbling fountain jets.

## 9. Ornamentation & Folk Details
* **Bas-Relief Panels**: Outer vertical walls of the octagonal basin feature stylized terracotta relief motifs depicting rural Bengal flora, lotus flowers, and river waves.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Sculpture Material**: Cast stone / reinforced cement with off-white smooth plaster coating and subtle grey-beige wash ($0.65$ Roughness, `#D6D1C7`).
* **Basin Perimeter**: Red brick coping and painted concrete curb.
* **Surrounding Lamps**: 4 heritage cast-iron single-lantern colonial street posts (`SM_Curzon_Lamp_Colonial`).

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Roadway Island**: Landscaped flowerbeds around the fountain base with yellow marigolds and green ground-cover shrubs.
* **Perimeter Canopy**: Flanked by majestic Neem and Krishnachura trees on the southern approach from Curzon Hall.
