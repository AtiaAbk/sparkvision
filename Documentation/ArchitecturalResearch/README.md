## 1. Executive Purpose & Research Standard

This directory houses the comprehensive **Architectural Research Atlas** for **SparkVision: University of Dhaka**. Every major campus building, public monument, memorial sculpture, and landscape element is documented with verified historical, geodetic, structural, and material ground truth.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All 3D modeling and environment assembly in SparkVision must strictly adhere to the specifications established in this atlas. We do not invent architectural styles, fantasy towers, or unhistorical geometry. Real photographic and geodetic ground truth governs all game assets.

## 3. Methodological Distinctions

Every document in this atlas strictly differentiates:
1. **Verified Facts**: Backed by authoritative documentation, direct ground photography, and verified cartography (e.g., Shaheed Minar's 14m mother pillar and 15° slant; Curzon Hall's 1904 foundation by Lord Curzon).
2. **Evidence-Supported Interpretations**: Proportions derived from photographic photogrammetry and architectural bay counting (e.g., Curzon's 6 modular bays east and west of the central 16m portal).
3. **Estimated Measurements**: Approximate widths derived from OpenStreetMap polygon boundaries when measured blueprints are in closed archives.
4. **Current In-Engine Approximations**: Documented simplifications (such as exterior-only shells for active classroom wings) reserved for Level 1 outdoor exploration.
