# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: KALA BHABAN (ARTS FACULTY)

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Western university campus, facing Mall Road and fronted by Aparajeyo Bangla.
* **Functional Role**: Primary building of the Faculty of Arts and Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Dhaka.

## 2. Geographic Coordinates
* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.733800° N, 90.391400° E`
* **Metric CRS (UTM Zone 46N / EPSG:32646)**: Easting = `234,054.10 m`, Northing = `2,627,177.30 m`
* **Unreal Engine Location**: `(X = 52500, Y = -162000, Z = 20)`

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Length (East-West Linear Block)**: Approximately $120.0\text{ m} - 130.0\text{ m}$.
* **Width (North-South Depth)**: Approximately $35.0\text{ m} - 40.0\text{ m}$ across wings.
* **Height**: 4-storey educational structure, ground-to-roof height $\approx 16.5\text{ m} - 18.0\text{ m}$.
* **Classroom Bays**: Modular $6.0\text{ m} \times 8.0\text{ m}$ structural bay grid.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Mid-Century Institutional Modernism**.
* **Era**: Built in the mid-1960s to accommodate the massive post-partition expansion of humanities and social sciences departments.
* **Functionalism**: Direct expression of modular utility—long linear shaded corridors, repetitive rectangular fenestration, and wide external stair towers designed for rapid student turnover between lectures.
* **Political Crucible**: The wide front steps (*Battala*) and shaded ground floor verandas served as the launching point for almost every major democratic student movement from the 1960s through 1990.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Grand linear rectangular bar elevated on concrete pillars, forming a majestic horizontal backdrop directly behind the Aparajeyo Bangla sculpture.

## 6. Facade Arrangement
* **Repetitive Grid**: Strict, orderly rhythm of repetitive classroom windows with horizontal concrete sunshades (*chhajjas*).
* **Corridors**: Open-air shaded circulation verandas spanning the entire south elevation facing the campus lawn.

## 7. Roofline
* **Roof**: Flat continuous reinforced concrete terrace punctuated by raised concrete water tanks and elevator headhouses.

## 8. Windows & External Staircases
* **Windows**: Steel casement windows recessed beneath concrete lintels.
* **Prominent Stair Towers**: Two monumental external open-well concrete staircases anchored at the east and west extremities of the building.

## 9. Structural Columns & Ground Pilotis
* **Ground Floor Colonnade**: The ground floor features an expansive covered walkway under the building supported by heavy rectangular concrete columns.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Painted Plaster & Concrete**: Light cream / off-white painted plaster over brickwork ($0.80$ Roughness, `#D9D4CC`).
* **Raw Concrete Trim**: Unpainted fair-faced concrete on exterior stairs and window hoods.
* **Terrazzo Floors**: Traditional green/grey polished terrazzo in corridors.

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Battala (Historic Banyan Tree)**: Historic leafy Banyan tree located directly adjacent to the Arts Faculty entrance porch.
* **Front Lawn**: Flat manicured grassy lawn separating the building from Mall Road.
