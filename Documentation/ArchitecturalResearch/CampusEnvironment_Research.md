# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT & BOTANICAL LANDSCAPE

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: University of Dhaka core campus enclave, encompassing the exploration corridor between Curzon Hall (South-East) and Arts Faculty / TSC (North-West).
* **Geographic Extent**: Approximately $1.6\text{ km} \times 0.9\text{ km}$ ($160,000\text{ cm} \times 90,000\text{ cm}$ in Unreal Units).

## 2. Road Network & Spatial Axes
* **Primary Axis (University Avenue)**: Connects Curzon Hall to Doyel Chattar and Shaheed Minar ($14\text{m}$ width, dual-lane asphalt).
* **Secondary Axis (Fuller Road & Mall Road)**: Tree-canopied scenic avenue connecting Shaheed Minar past the British Council to TSC and Arts Faculty.
* **Curzon Promenade**: Traditional herringbone brick-paved ceremonial pedestrian promenade ($10\text{m}$ width) linking the botanical pond to the central portico.

## 3. Indigenous Flora & Botanical Species
* **Rain Tree (*Samanea saman*)**: Majestic giant shade trees with expansive horizontal umbrella canopies spanning up to $25\text{ m} - 35\text{ m}$ across, lining Fuller Road and Curzon lawns.
* **Banyan Tree (*Ficus benghalensis*)**: Sacred and historical multi-trunk canopy trees with aerial prop roots (notably *Battala* at Arts Faculty and around Mall field).
* **Krishnachura / Flamboyant (*Delonix regia*)**: Spreading deciduous trees with intense scarlet/orange blossoms, blooming during early summer and late spring.
* **Neem (*Azadirachta indica*) & Debdaru (*Polyalthia longifolia*)**: Upright columnar trees lining academic compound walls and pedestrian footpaths.
* **Curzon Water Lilies (*Nymphaea nouchali*)**: Pink and white water lilies inhabiting the Curzon Hall botanical pond.

## 4. Street Furniture & Public Fixtures
* **Colonial Cast-Iron Benches**: Slatted dark teak wood seats supported by ornamental cast-iron scroll armrests and legs (`SM_Curzon_Bench_Colonial`).
* **Heritage Street Lamps**: Fluted cast-iron poles with hexagonal glass lantern crowns (`SM_Curzon_Lamp_Colonial`).
* **Campus Tea Stalls (*Tong-er Dokan*)**: Historic wooden/tin student gathering stalls with wooden benches, copper kettles, and ceramic tea cups.
* **Notice Boards & Signposts**: Metal frame notice boards displaying student cultural hand-painted posters (*Chhobir Haat* style).

## 5. Paving Materials & Ground Textures
* **Road Asphalt**: Weathered dark-grey bitumen asphalt with subtle aggregate chipping and faded white centerlines (`MI_Campus_Asphalt`).
* **Herringbone Brick Walkways**: Terracotta brick pavers laid in classic 45° herringbone patterns along Curzon and Shaheed Minar approaches.
* **Lawn Turf**: Subtropical Bermuda grass mixed with patches of moist Bengal soil (`MI_Campus_LawnGrass`).

## 6. Atmosphere, Weather & Lighting Benchmarks
* **Tropical Natural Daylight**: Clear, crisp afternoon sunlight ($5800\text{ K}$) with soft atmospheric haze and ambient skylight bounces.
* **Golden Hour (Cinematic Benchmark)**: Warm low-angle sun ($3500\text{ K}$) casting long dramatic shadows through tree canopies onto historic red-brick facades, directly inspired by *Ghost of Tsushima*.
* **Monsoon Mist**: Volumetric ground mist rolling off the botanical pond and lawns in early morning or after heavy monsoon downpours, echoing the moody depth of *Where Winds Meet*.

## 7. Natural Boundaries & Flow Barriers
* **Colonial Boundary Walls**: Low ($1.2\text{ m}$) red-brick walls capped with half-round brick coping.
* **Wrought-Iron Railings**: Vintage black spearhead iron fences separating university plots from high-security institutional sectors (Supreme Court, Medical College).
* **Dense Hedgerows**: Well-tended bougainvillea and privet hedges bounding botanical garden sectors without requiring artificial collision walls.
