# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: TEACHER-STUDENT CENTRE (TSC)

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Central university intersection ("TSC Mor"), University of Dhaka campus, Shahbagh, Dhaka.
* **Functional Role**: Social, cultural, and political epicenter of Dhaka University student life.

## 2. Geographic Coordinates
* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.731900° N, 90.392700° E`
* **Metric CRS (UTM Zone 46N / EPSG:32646)**: Easting = `234,188.75 m`, Northing = `2,626,968.32 m`
* **Unreal Engine Location**: `(X = 43000, Y = -149000, Z = 20)` (~1,550m North-West of Curzon Hall)

## 3. Building Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Footprint**: Expansive multi-wing pavilion complex spanning approximately $90.0\text{ m} \times 70.0\text{ m}$.
* **Building Height**: Low-rise two-storey structure, ground-to-roof height $\approx 8.5\text{ m} - 9.5\text{ m}$ to preserve human scale.
* **Central Courtyard (*Uthan*)**: Open rectangular grass courtyard $\approx 45.0\text{ m} \times 35.0\text{ m}$.
* **Butterfly Canopy**: Projecting double-roof canopy over the cafeteria entrance spanning $\approx 18.0\text{ m} \times 12.0\text{ m}$.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Tropical Regional Modernism**, designed by renowned Greek architect and urban theorist **Constantinos Apostolou Doxiadis** in 1961.
* **Philosophy**: Rejection of cold industrial modernism in favor of local contextual responsiveness: incorporating the Bengal rural courtyard (*uthan*) typology, passive ventilation, and shaded gathering zones.
* **Historical Significance**: The central staging ground for the 1969 Mass Uprising, 1971 Liberation War student mobilization, and anti-authoritarian democracy movements.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: Horizontal, grounded, low-slung modernist profile punctuated by deep cantilevered roof planes, horizontal window ribbons, and open colonnades.
* **Contrasting Character**: Radically distinct from the colonial red-brick ornamentation of Curzon Hall; celebrates structural concrete clarity.

## 6. Facade Arrangement
* **Grid**: Exposed reinforced concrete post-and-beam frame with terracotta brick infill panels.
* **Rhythm**: Alternating solid brick walls and deep continuous horizontal louver ribbons.

## 7. Roofline
* **Roof Planes**: Flat reinforced concrete slabs with projecting overhangs and the signature elevated **butterfly canopy** providing an air cushion for natural cooling.

## 8. Windows & Entrance Design
* **Shading**: Deep concrete brise-soleil (sun-breakers) and recessed window bands preventing direct solar heat gain.
* **Entrance**: Wide, inviting open-air foyer leading directly from the street into the inner courtyard.

## 9. Columns, Arches, Verandas & Ornamentation
* **Columns**: Slender cylindrical and rectangular reinforced concrete pilotis.
* **Amphitheatre**: Outdoor tiered semicircular Greek-style concrete seating integrated into the southern courtyard.
* **Ornamentation**: Strictly architectural shadow play, exposed aggregate textures, and honest material expression; zero applied classical decoration.

## 10. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Fair-Faced Concrete**: Board-formed light grey concrete ($0.75$ Roughness, `#A8A39D`).
* **Terracotta Brick Infill**: Warm earthen red brick ($0.85$ Roughness).
* **Metalwork**: Slender black steel window mullions and railings.

## 11. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Courtyard Greenery**: Manicured lawn (*TSC Math*) dotted with royal palm trees and flowering shrubs.
* **Roundabout Context**: Faces the busy circular roundabout where the Raju Memorial Sculpture stands.
