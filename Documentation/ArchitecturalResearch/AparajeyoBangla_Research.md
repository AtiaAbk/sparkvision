# ARCHITECTURAL RESEARCH ATLAS: APARAJEYO BANGLA

## 1. Verified Location
* **Location**: Prominently situated in the main front courtyard directly before the Arts Faculty (Kala Bhaban) building, western campus sector.
* **Functional Role**: Monumental tribute to the freedom fighters (*Mukti Joddha*) of the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War; historic rallying point for democracy rallies.

* **WGS 84 (GPS)**: `23.733500° N, 90.391600° E`

## 3. Dimensions & Physical Scale
* **Total Height**: Approximately **5.5 meters (18.0 feet)** from ground to highest point, including the architectural base.
* **Sculpted Figures Height**: Life-and-a-half monumental scale ($\approx 3.3\text{ m} - 3.5\text{ m}$ figure height).
* **Pedestal Base**: Rectilinear reinforced concrete plinth, footprint $\approx 6.0\text{ m} \times 4.5\text{ m}$, height $\approx 1.8\text{ m} - 2.0\text{ m}$.

## 4. Architectural Style & Historical Background
* **Style**: **Heroic Figurative Modernist Sculpture**.
* **Sculptor**: Designed and sculpted by freedom fighter and renowned artist **Syed Abdullah Khalid**.
* **Base Architect**: Base structural design by prominent architect **Rabioul Hossain**.
* **Chronology**: Begun in 1973 under DUCSU leadership; completed and formally unveiled on **December 16, 1979** (Victory Day).
* **Name Etymology**: Named "Aparajeyo Bangla" (*"Unvanquished Bengal"*) following an influential 1970s investigative article by journalist Saleh Chowdhury in *Dainik Bangla*.

## 5. Main Silhouette & Massing
* **Silhouette**: A powerful three-figure triangular composition radiating unstoppable forward momentum, resolve, and collective sacrifice.

## 6. The Three Figures & Symbolic Composition
1. **The Peasant / Farmer (Center)**:
   - Stands boldly in the center with a rifle slung over his shoulder, holding a hand grenade poised in his right hand.
   - Represents the rural masses and farmer fighters who formed the backbone of the Mukti Bahini.
   - Model: Badrul Alam Benu (student of Art College).
2. **The Student / Youth (Right)**:
   - Sturdy young man in casual trousers holding an automatic rifle firmly across his chest with alert posture.
   - Represents the educated student guerrillas who spearheaded urban resistance.
   - Model: Syed Hamid Maksood (Dhaka University student).
3. **The Woman / Nurse (Left)**:
   - Resolute Bengali woman holding a first-aid medicine box, gazing vigilantly into the horizon.
   - Represents the indispensable courage and battlefield medical service of Bengali women.
   - Model: Hasina Ahmed (housewife and supporter of the liberation struggle).

## 7. Base & Plinth Design
* **Stepped Base**: Multi-tiered fair-faced concrete plinth providing a durable podium that elevates the figures above student assemblies.

## 8. Surface Texture & Chisel Work
* **Chiseled Concrete**: Coarse bush-hammered concrete surface texture showcasing aggregate chips and sculptor's hand-chisel strokes, giving the monument raw, timeless vitality.

## 9. Materials & Surface Characteristics
* **Core & Skin**: Cast reinforced concrete (*Ferrocement*) with stone aggregate ($0.85$ Roughness, `#A39F99` weathered concrete grey).
* **Patina**: Faint natural mineral weathering streaks and rain channels accumulated over 45+ years.

## 10. Surrounding Landscape & Vegetation
* **Kala Bhaban Plaza**: Expansive brick-paved concourse connecting the monument to the entrance steps of the Arts Faculty.
* **Canopy Trees**: Framed by tall rain trees and shady Debdaru (false ashoka) trees providing a verdant backdrop.
