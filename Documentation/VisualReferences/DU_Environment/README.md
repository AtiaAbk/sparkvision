# DU ENVIRONMENT, LANDSCAPE & CAMPUS ATMOSPHERE REFERENCES

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This directory collects authentic visual references documenting the **landscape, outdoor environment, roads, flora, street furniture, and seasonal atmosphere** across the University of Dhaka campus.

While `DU_Architecture/` focuses on building structures, this directory provides the grounding for:
- Road surfacing, curbs, and pedestrian pavement textures.
- Indigenous tree species, canopies, and garden flowerbeds.
- Public furniture (benches, lamp posts, signage, tea stalls).
- Campus atmosphere across weather and times of day.

---

## 2. Key Environmental Subject Categories

### 2.1 Roads, Paths & Circulation
* **University Avenue (Fuller Road & Mall Road)**: Two-lane asphalt roads lined with mature rain trees and shaded pedestrian footpaths.
* **Curzon Promenade**: Traditional herringbone red-brick paving (`MI_Curzon_Brick`) running between the botanical pond and front portico.
* **Curbs & Drainage**: Weathered concrete curbs, brick gutters, and drainage grates common across campus.

### 2.2 Flora, Trees & Greenery
* **Hero Trees**: Giant Rain Trees (*Samanea saman*), Banyan / Krishnachura (*Delonix regia*) with scarlet blossoms, and Neem trees lining Curzon and Fuller Road.
* **Botanical Gardens & Ponds**: Curzon Hall botanical pond with water lilies, stone borders, and lush surrounding shrubbery.
* **Lawn Grass & Shrubs**: Tropical Bermuda grass, hedge trimmings, and decorative flowerbeds in Curzon quadrangle and TSC garden.

### 2.3 Campus Furniture & Micro-Props
* **Heritage Lighting**: Colonial cast-iron post lamps along Curzon promenade; modern sodium / LED streetlights along University Avenue.
* **Benches**: Teak-slat benches with cast-iron ornamental armrests found in colonial zones; concrete park benches around Mall and Hakim Chattar.
* **Cultural Fixtures**: Historic campus tea stalls (Tong-er dokan), banner signposts, and notice boards.

### 2.4 Lighting & Atmospheric Quality
* **Monsoon & Fog**: Morning winter mist rolling across Curzon lawns; humid post-monsoon rain reflections on wet brick paving.
* **Golden Hour**: Low-angle afternoon sunlight cutting through dense tree canopies onto historic red-brick facades.
