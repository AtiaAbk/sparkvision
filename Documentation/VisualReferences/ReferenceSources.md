# SPARKVISION — VISUAL REFERENCE SOURCES REGISTER

## 1. Purpose & Standards

This document maintains an audited register of all external visual references, architectural documentation, photographs, and artistic inspiration sources collected for **SparkVision: University of Dhaka**.

> [!NOTE]
> All external reference materials must be cataloged here before being referenced in visual baselines. In accordance with strict development standards, source fields must remain blank if verified metadata is not yet available. Do not invent source URLs, author attributions, or licensing terms.

## 2. Primary Verified Reference Repositories

The following primary authorities are used for authentic geographic, architectural, and visual presentation research:

1. **University of Dhaka Official Website**
   * **URL**: [https://www.du.ac.bd/](https://www.du.ac.bd/)
   * **Scope**: Administrative history, faculty buildings, institutional identity, and official building designations.

2. **University of Dhaka Official Campus Map**
   * **URL**: [https://old.du.ac.bd/footer_widget/footer_widget_item/map](https://old.du.ac.bd/footer_widget/footer_widget_item/map)
   * **Scope**: Definitive master layout of campus plots, university boundary markers, road alignments, and named quadrangles.

3. **OpenStreetMap (University of Dhaka Campus Area)**
   * **URL**: [https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=17/23.7280/90.3975](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=17/23.7280/90.3975)
   * **Scope**: Geodetic road networks, pedestrian walkways, building footprints, and GPS coordinates for UTM Zone 46N reprojection.

4. **Wikimedia Commons: Curzon Hall Category**
   * **URL**: [https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Curzon_Hall](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Curzon_Hall)
   * **Scope**: High-resolution, multi-angle historical and modern photographs of Curzon Hall facade, verandas, chhatris, brickwork, and botanical gardens.

5. **Where Winds Meet (Everstone Studio / NetEase Games)**
   * **URL**: [https://www.wherewindsmeetgame.com/](https://www.wherewindsmeetgame.com/)
   * **Scope**: Visual presentation reference for atmospheric depth, volumetric mist, vegetation interaction, and semi-open-world third-person framing.

6. **Ghost of Tsushima (Sucker Punch Productions / Sony Interactive Entertainment)**
   * **URL**: [https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/ghost-of-tsushima/](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/ghost-of-tsushima/)
   * **Scope**: Visual presentation reference for cinematic lighting, golden-hour directional contrast, landscape layering, and scenic guiding routes.
