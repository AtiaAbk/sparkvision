# GHOST OF TSUSHIMA — LANDSCAPE & CINEMATIC COMPOSITION BENCHMARKS

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This directory contains visual presentation benchmarks derived from *Ghost of Tsushima* (Sucker Punch Productions). These references guide SparkVision's **landscape composition, directional sunlight, atmospheric color grading, and organic scene framing**.

> [!NOTE]
> *Ghost of Tsushima* is referenced strictly for its world-renowned mastery of natural lighting, high-contrast silhouette framing, organic route guidance, and cinematic color palettes. All cultural, architectural, and geographic designs in SparkVision remain strictly faithful to Dhaka University.

---

## 2. Key Artistic Benchmarks

### 2.1 Foreground, Middle-Ground & Background Layering
* **Depth Creation**: Establishing clear 3-point depth in every outdoor framing:
  - **Foreground**: Brick balustrades, tree branches, colonial lamp posts, or floral shrubs close to camera.
  - **Middle-Ground**: The primary playable space, player character, monument porticos, and road avenues.
  - **Background**: Distant tree canopies, horizon silhouettes, and soft atmospheric haze.

### 2.2 Cinematic Sunlight & Golden Hour Contrast
* **Low Sun Angle (Golden Hour)**: Warm directional sunlight ($3200\text{ K} - 4000\text{ K}$) hitting terracotta red bricks at a shallow angle, creating long dramatic shadows across the Curzon promenade and lawns.
* **Warm and Cool Color Contrasts**: Warm brick highlights balanced against cool blue ambient skylight in shaded verandas and under tree canopies.

### 2.3 Scenic Exploration Routes & Visual Guiding
* **Natural Landmarks as Beacons**: Using monumental silhouettes (Curzon's corner towers, Shaheed Minar's mother pillar, Raju Sculpture) to orient the player organically without intrusive HUD markers.
* **Road & Tree Corridors**: Framing roads like University Avenue with arching tree branches that draw the eye toward destination landmarks.

### 2.4 Camera Composition & Stillness
* **Atmospheric Stillness**: Balancing majestic architectural scale against organic environmental motion (leaf flutter, subtle mist roll, water ripples in Curzon pond).
