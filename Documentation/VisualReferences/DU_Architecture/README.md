# DU ARCHITECTURAL REFERENCE PHOTOGRAPHS & REPERTORY

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This directory is **exclusively reserved for authentic architectural reference photographs** of the University of Dhaka. It forms the primary ground truth for all 3D mesh modeling, modular architectural kits, texture authoring, and proportions in Unreal Engine 5.8.

---

## 2. Architectural Focus Areas & Landmark Categories

All photos in this directory must be classified and organized into the following architectural components:

### 2.1 Curzon Hall Complex (1904 Indo-Saracenic Hero Building)
* **Front, Rear & Side Elevations**: Orthographic and perspective photographs of the full 150m south facade, northern garden rear entrance, and east/west wings.
* **Monumental Portico & Entrance**: Multi-angle views of the 16m-wide, 2-storey central entrance portal, including cusped arch rims, sandstone imposts, string courses, and stepped parapets.
* **Two-Tiered Verandas**: Clear close-ups of ground-floor rusticated arches and upper-floor multi-foil cusped arches, paired octagonal pillars, lotus capitals, and perforated jali railings.
* **Corner Towers & Chhatris**: Authentic details of the corner octagonal towers, 8-pillared domed kiosks (chhatris), bracket supports, ribbed hemispherical roofs, and metal finials.
* **Roofline Truth**: Archival confirmation of the flat roof with ornamental parapets, definitively proving the **absence of an oversized central dome**.
* **Brickwork & Stonework**: Macro photography of historic red-brick masonry, mortar lines, weathering patterns, and contrasting white sandstone lintels.

### 2.2 Teacher-Student Centre (TSC)
* **Exterior Architecture**: Modernist concrete design by Greek architect Konstantinos Doxiadis (1960s), including the prominent cantilevers, horizontal louvers, interior courtyard, and Greek amphitheatre.

### 2.3 Central Library & Hakim Chattar
* **Colonial & Modernist Blend**: Multi-storey library facade, brick-and-concrete pilasters, historic reading hall windows, and surrounding perimeter paving.

### 2.4 Shaheed Minar (National Language Monument)
* **Monumental Proportions**: 14-meter-tall central slanted marble mother pillar, crimson circular sun disc, four flanking child columns, and stepped marble altar platform.

### 2.5 Aparajeyo Bangla & Arts Faculty (Kala Bhaban)
* **Sculptural Monument**: Close-up proportions of Syed Abdullah Khalid's three-figure liberation monument (freedom fighters).
* **Arts Faculty Building**: Linear modernist concrete facade, external staircase columns, and shaded verandas.

### 2.6 Doyel Chattar & Kazi Nazrul Islam Mausoleum
* **Doyel Fountain**: Octagonal stone basin and central interlocking oriental magpie-robin (Doyel) birds.
* **Nazrul Mausoleum**: Open-air marble cenotaph beside the DU Central Mosque, jali screen perimeter, and floral planters.
