# SPARKVISION — ART DIRECTION & VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

## 1. Title & Executive Purpose

* **Document**: `ArtDirection.md`
* **Purpose**: Defines the aesthetic vision, atmospheric mood, color theory, and architectural language of **SparkVision: University of Dhaka**.

---

## 2. Aesthetic Pillars

1. **Authentic Bengal Heritage**: Grounded in authentic red-brick masonry, white sandstone string courses, cusped arches, and lush subtropical flora of Dhaka.
2. **Cinematic Environmental Depth**: Inspired by *Where Winds Meet* and *Ghost of Tsushima*, environments feature rich atmospheric mist, natural light filtration through giant tree canopies, and organic ground transitions.
3. **Contrast of Warm and Cool**: Warm sunlit brick facades juxtaposed against cool, shaded verandas, deep-green lawns, and tranquil botanical water surfaces.

## 4. Architectural Motifs (Indo-Saracenic Revival)

* **Cusped Multi-Foil Arches**: Elegant 7-lobe and 9-lobe pointed arches adorning Curzon Hall verandas.
* **Chhajjas (Projecting Eaves)**: Continuous sloped stone drip-molds supported by carved brackets protecting lower verandas from monsoon rains.
* **Chhatris (Domed Kiosks)**: Octagonal pillared open-air pavilions with ribbed hemispherical domes and vertical finials capping towers and porticos.
* **Jali Balustrades**: Intricate geometric openwork screen railings along second-storey balconies.
