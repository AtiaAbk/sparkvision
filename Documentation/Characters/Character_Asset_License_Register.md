### 1. License Policy & Compliance Standard
SparkVision strictly enforces copyright integrity. No pirated, unverified, or ambiguous third-party 3D models or textures are permitted in the project. All character assets, textures, rigs, and animations must be:
1. Native Unreal Engine standard project distribution assets (Epic Games Content License).
2. Procedurally generated original PBR textures created specifically for SparkVision.
3. Permissive CC0 or CC-BY licensed models properly documented with attribution.
