# SparkVision — Character Research Master
## Authentic University of Dhaka & Bangladesh Campus Character & Attire Research (July 2024 Period)

### 1. Executive Context & Cultural Background
The University of Dhaka (DU, established 1921), revered as the "Oxford of the East" and the cradle of Bangladesh's historic language, independence, and democracy movements, represents a vibrant, culturally rich, and highly varied demographic cross-section of Bangladeshi youth, academics, staff, law enforcement, and civil society. In July 2024, the campus was at the heart of historic student assemblies, peaceful marches, dialogue forums, and civic defense across Curzon Hall, the Central Shaheed Minar, the Teacher-Student Centre (TSC), Raju Memorial Sculpture, and Kala Bhaban (Faculty of Arts).

This document establishes the authentic photographic, sociological, and architectural wardrobe standards for all character groups within SparkVision, eliminating generic fantasy, anachronistic combat uniforms, or inappropriate martial-arts garb.

---

### 2. Character Archetypes & Attire Taxonomy

#### 2.1 Main Playable Protagonist (Hero Wardrobe)
* **Archetype**: University of Dhaka student leader / researcher.
  - **Option A (Male Hero - "Shovon" / Curzon Science)**:
    - **Torso**: Authentic University of Dhaka signature deep maroon (`#7A141F`) breathable combed-cotton student shirt / polo. Tailored rolled sleeves, subtle Dhaka University crest / gold-tinged student badge on breast pocket.
    - **Legwear**: Classic indigo washed denim (`#142652`), durable double-stitched cotton-poly blend, rolled cuffs suited for rapid campus movement across Curzon brick pathways and wet seasonal grass.
    - **Footwear**: Sturdy ergonomic canvas sneakers / breathable sports trainers (`#2B2B2B` with white trim).
    - **Accessories**: Crossbody canvas messenger bag (`#3A3022`), student ID lanyard with DU seal, leather-strap field watch.
  - **Option B (Female Hero - "Rina" / Biochemistry & TSC)**:
    - **Torso & Tunic**: Elegant pastel mint-sage or terracotta embroidered cotton kurti / salwar kameez with light floral hem detail (`#3A6D63` / `#D48C70`).
    - **Legwear & Dupatta**: Breathable white / cream cotton palazzo trousers (`#EAE6DD`) and draped lightweight georgette dupatta / orna pinned securely across shoulder.
    - **Footwear**: Ergonomic flat leather sandals or low-profile sports sneakers.
    - **Accessories**: Canvas shoulder tote bag, Dhaka University science faculty ID card, wristband.

#### 2.2 Male University Students
* **Everyday Student Varieties**:
  1. *Traditional Festive / Friday Student*: Pure handspun white cotton Panjabi (`#EBEAE5`) with mother-of-pearl buttons and mandarin collar, paired with crisp white cotton pajama or dark denim.
  2. *Science / Engineering Student*: Sky-blue or navy short-sleeve button-down Oxford shirt (`#224477`), rolled cuffs, khaki chinos (`#9E8867`), sturdy brown leather loafers or slip-on sneakers.
  3. *Arts & Social Science Student*: Charcoal or olive green casual cotton crewneck T-shirt (`#353B3C`), relaxed charcoal jeans (`#2E3138`), canvas backpack.
  4. *Active Union Member*: Crimson / maroon polo shirt with collegiate white collars, indigo denim, sports trainers.

#### 2.3 Female University Students
* **Authentic Varieties**:
  1. *Classic Salwar Kameez*: Pastel lavender / sky blue two-piece stitched cotton ensemble with matching patterned dupatta (`#6E5A8A` / `#82A7C9`).
  2. *Modern Contemporary Kurti & Denim*: Olive green or mustard yellow long-line tunic kurti (`#8B7500` / `#486045`) paired with slim-fit indigo jeans and lightweight scarf.
  3. *Hijab Student Variant*: Graceful soft jersey / chiffon hijab in navy or rose-tan, coordinated with modest long tunic and neutral trousers.
  4. *Campus Commuter*: Printed block-print cotton top, dark trousers, sturdy leather flats, college backpack.

#### 2.4 University Faculty & Teachers
* **Distinguished Academic Attire**:
  - *Male Professor / Senior Lecturer*: Semi-formal crisp beige / grey half-sleeve or full-sleeve buttoned safari shirt or formal check shirt (`#78766F`), tailored dark grey trousers (`#32353B`), black leather Oxford shoes, leather briefcase or folder folio, wire-rimmed glasses.
  - *Female Professor / Senior Faculty*: Traditional Bangladeshi handloom cotton or Dhakai Jamdani / Tangail saree with contrast border and pinned anchal, or formal long kurti with formal dupatta, elegant low heels or leather sandals.

#### 2.5 Campus Staff & Technical Officers
* **Administrative & Support Personnel**:
  - *Lab Demonstrator / Library Staff*: Practical short-sleeve check cotton shirt in slate-blue or forest green, practical brown trousers, utility pen clip in front pocket, campus staff identification card.
  - *Maintenance & Grounds Staff*: Durable khaki or olive drab twill two-piece utility work shirt and utility trousers, rubber boots or slip-on utility sandals.

#### 2.6 Campus Security & Proctorial Personnel
* **University Proctorial Team**:
  - *Proctorial Guard*: Khaki safari-cut utility uniform (`#7D6D4E`), dark epaulets, brass university security badge on left chest, black web belt, dark beret or peak cap, polished black combat shoes / boots.

#### 2.7 Mission 6 Hostile Opponent ("Cadre / Agitator" Faction)
* **Fictional Non-Student Agitators**:
  - Visual distinction is paramount: ordinary students wear open, welcoming attire; hostile agitators wear concealing motorcycle gear to prevent identification.
  - *Headgear*: Full-face or three-quarter matte-black / dark crimson motorcycle helmets with tinted visors (`#1A1A1A` / `#6E1515`).
  - *Torso*: Dark synthetic windbreaker, leather motorcycle jacket, or heavy black/grey graphic T-shirt (`#202224`).
  - *Legwear*: Heavy faded black / dark denim jeans (`#1D1E22`), reinforced utility belts.
  - *Accessories / Weapons*: Hardwood lathi / baton (`#5C3A21`), motorcycle gloves.

#### 2.8 Bangladesh Police Service
* **Period-Accurate Police Service Attire (July 2024)**:
  - *Standard Duty Uniform*: Authentic dual-tone regulation uniform — pale lavender/greyish-blue short-sleeve cotton uniform shirt (`#68788C` / `#748396`) with police crest patch and dark navy rank insignia, paired with deep navy blue regulation trousers (`#152038`).
  - *Duty Belt & Footwear*: Heavy black leather Sam Browne duty belt with brass buckle and holster, high-shine regulation black leather tactical boots (`#111111`).
  - *Headgear*: Deep navy police beret with metal Bangladesh Police monogram crest.

### 3. Rigging, Skeleton & Performance Specifications
* **Engine & Skeleton**: Unreal Engine 5.8 Skeleton (`/Game/Characters/Mannequins/Meshes/SK_Mannequin`).
* **Skeletal Meshes**: Standardized on Manny (`SKM_Manny_Simple`) and Quinn (`SKM_Quinn_Simple`) shared bone hierarchy.
* **Physics Asset**: `PA_Mannequin` ragdoll setup.
* **Animation System**: `ABP_Unarmed` shared anim blueprint supporting idle, walk, run, sprint, jump, double jump, wall jump, and directional evasion.
* **Memory Optimization**: 16 GB unified memory target (MacBook Air M4) achieved through modular PBR Material Instances (`MI_Student_*`, `MI_Police_*`, `MI_Cadre_*`) driving shared textures and parent shaders without duplicating 50 MB geometry buffers per NPC.
