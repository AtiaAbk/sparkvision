## 2. Sectoral Structural Analysis & Architectural Identity

### Sector 1: Curzon & Science Precinct
* **Curzon Hall (`STR-01`)**:
  - *Silhouette*: Stepped vertical profile with two prominent 25.2m corner towers and a central double-height entrance portal topped with twin chatris.
  - *Roof Features*: Rajput open-pillared kiosks (*chatris*) with hemispherical white cupolas and inverted lotus finials; stepped Mughal merlons along the parapet wall.
  - *Facade & Colonnade*: Continuous double-tiered verandas with pointed and cusped horse-shoe arches, octagonal red-brick pillars with white stone capitals, and carved geometric jali screens.
  - *Materiality*: Rich red-oxide terracotta brickwork contrasted against dressed white sandstone trim, arches, and cornices.
* **Curzon Botanical Pond (`STR-02`)**:
  - *Structure*: Formal rectangular perennial water basin (55m × 40m) bordered by dressed stone coping. Features southern ghat steps for student relaxation.
* **Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah Hall (`STR-05`)**:
  - *Layout*: Classic 1921 British colonial academic quadrangle. Two-to-three-storey red-brick perimeter wings enclosing a lush central courtyard garden.

### Sector 2: University Avenue & Doyel Chattar
* **Doyel Chattar Roundabout (`STR-06`)**:
  - *Monuments*: Two back-to-back stylized Magpie-Robin birds (*Copsychus saularis*) in patinated dark bronze with cocked fan tails.
  - *Base*: Stepped concentric fountain basin with active reflective water surface and decorative perimeter curb.
* **Bangla Academy (`STR-07`)**:
  - *Structure*: Centered around historic Burdwan House—a two-storey colonial residence featuring wide shaded verandas, semicircular arches, and deep window louvers.

### Sector 3: Memorial & Residential Corridor
* **Central Shaheed Minar (`STR-09`)**:
  - *Central Monument*: 14.0m Mother Pillar inclined at 15° forward, symbolizing maternal protection over fallen language martyrs.
  - *Sun Disc*: 5.2m vertical crimson disc placed behind the mother pillar.
  - *Altar*: Expansive 32m × 22m stepped white marble terrace with black granite border trim and iron handrails.
* **Salimullah Muslim Hall (`STR-11`)**:
  - *Layout*: Fortified 1931 Indo-Saracenic quadrangle on a 12.91-acre campus estate.
  - *Gatehouse*: Symmetrical southern portal flanked by twin square towers capped with distinctive yellow domes.
  - *Corners*: Four bold corner projections crowned with triple domed chhatris.

### Sector 4: TSC & Central Library Precinct
* **Teacher-Student Centre (`STR-14`)**:
  - *Architectural Style*: Constantinos Doxiadis's tropical modernism (1961).
  - *Auditorium*: Hexagonal drum with cantilevered folded-plate concrete roof.
  - *Breezeway*: Ground-level open pilotis allowing constant natural air circulation.
* **Anti-Terrorism Raju Memorial Sculpture (`STR-15`)**:
  - *Sculpture*: Circular ring of 8 linked student figures with hands clasped in defiance, sculpted by Mahmudul Hasan Shohag in 1997.
  - *Pedestal*: Three-tier concentric circular dark granite plinth (14m base diameter).
* **DU Central Library (`STR-16`)**:
  - *Architectural Style*: Muzharul Islam's seminal tropical modernism (1953).
  - *Facade*: Elevated on structural pilotis; deep vertical concrete brise-soleil fins to deflect harsh solar heat while admitting diffuse daylight.
* **DU Central Mosque & Nazrul Tomb (`STR-18`, `STR-19`)**:
  - *Mosque*: Large white hemispherical dome and 4 slender corner minarets overlooking the grand lawn.
  - *Mausoleum*: Open-air white marble cenotaph with delicate jali perimeter screens nestled beside the mosque in accordance with the poet's song.

### Sector 5: Arts, Social Sciences & Western Quad
* **Arts Faculty / Kala Bhaban (`STR-20`)**:
  - *Massing*: Monumental 160m rectilinear block oriented along the East-West axis.
  - *Sun Shading*: Deep horizontal concrete ribbon louvers across all upper floors.
* **Aparajeyo Bangla (`STR-21`)**:
  - *Sculpture*: 11.2m monumental cast-concrete trio by Syed Abdullah Khalid (1979)—peasant freedom fighter, female nurse/patriot, and university student.
* **Surja Sen & Mohsin Halls (`STR-23`)**:
  - *Massing*: Modernist repetitive residential blocks with deep modular window louvers and internal lightwells.
