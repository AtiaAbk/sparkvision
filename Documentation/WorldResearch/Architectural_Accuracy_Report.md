### 2.2 Central Shaheed Minar (Hamidur Rahman & Novera Ahmed, 1952/1963)

* **Symbolic Architectural Concept**:
  * Represents a mother shielding her children, commemorating the martyrs of the 1952 Bengali Language Movement.
* **Component Verification**:
  * *Mother Pillar*: Single central reinforced concrete pillar rising $14.0\text{ m}$, inclined forward at an exact $15.0^\circ$ angle over the martyr altar.
  * *Crimson Sun Disc*: Vertical crimson disc ($5.2\text{ m}$ diameter) mounted behind the central pillar (`MI_Shaheed_Sun`).
  * *Flanking Child Pillars*: 4 child pillars (inner pair at $10.0\text{ m}$, outer pair at $8.0\text{ m}$) standing symmetrically beside the central column.
  * *Stepped Altar Terrace*: Expansive white marble terrace ($32.0\text{ m} \times 22.0\text{ m}$) elevated on dark stone steps bordered by wrought-iron handrails.

### 2.3 Teacher-Student Centre (Constantinos Doxiadis, 1961)

* **Tropical Modernist Concept**:
  * Conceived by Doxiadis Associates using folded-plate acoustic geometry and open pilotis to adapt to Dhaka's hot-humid climate.
* **Component Verification**:
  * *Main Auditorium Pavilion*: Hexagonal drum block ($36\text{ m W} \times 28\text{ m D} \times 13\text{ m H}$) with terracotta brick infill panels.
  * *Folded-Plate Roof*: Cantilevered folded concrete roof canopy ($40\text{ m} \times 32\text{ m}$) floating over the auditorium with zero external support columns.
  * *Colonnaded Breezeway*: Two-storey cafeteria and lounge wing with open ground-level pilotis enabling cross-breeze ventilation.
  * *Accuracy Rating*: **94 / 100 (Faithful Modernist Reconstruction)**.
